### Details that matter

**EPSS**  
- note: Use it to order, not to justify. A high EPSS does not mean your instance is reachable.

**SARIF 2.1.0**  
- note: If a finding does not fit SARIF, the customer cannot ingest it, and that is how reports get ignored.

**This repo: prompts/04-prove-it.md**  
- preconditions: `a candidate finding`
- oracle: `one of CONFIRMED, UNCONFIRMED, REFUTED, NEEDS_ACCESS`
- detected by: critic verdict OVERSTATED
- note: Local document. The rule it enforces: no CONFIRMED without a negative control.

**This repo: prompts/05-critic.md**  
- preconditions: `another agent's finding`
- oracle: `SOUND, OVERSTATED, UNSAFE_TO_REPORT or REFUTED`

**GDPR Article 33 breach notification clock**

## Decision tree

Machine readable at `trees/D24.json`. This is the rendered view.

- **Can you reproduce this finding from what you wrote down?**
  - _why:_ If the answer is no, nothing else in this domain matters. The test is brutal and it kills most reports.
- `No`
  - action: the finding is not ready. capture the exact request, the response, the control and the timestamp, then retry the question
  - tools: `d24-prove`
  - oracle: `a reproduction that a stranger can run`
- `Yes`
  - **Does it have a negative control?**
  - `No`
    - action: you have a hypothesis. run the control, or downgrade the claim
    - tools: `d24-critic`
    - oracle: `control behaves as predicted`
  - `Yes`
    - action: write it up: what state changed, as whom, how it persists, and what stops it
    - tools: `d24-cvss`, `d24-epss`, `d24-kev`, `d24-sarif`
    - oracle: `report maps to CWE and SARIF`
- `It is a known CVE`
  - action: check KEV first, then EPSS for ordering. do not use CVSS alone to prioritise
  - tools: `d24-kev`, `d24-epss`, `d24-cve`
  - oracle: `exploited status and probability`

## Defense mirror

The other half of every row. An offensive entry with no matching detection and control is
incomplete, because the practical question is always against which defence.

| attack | detect | control | rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| overstated finding | critic verdict OVERSTATED | mandatory critic pass before reporting | finding without control |
| irreproducible report | engineer cannot replay | verbatim request and response in the report | missing evidence path |
| scope breach in the report | finding outside the recorded scope | scope check before writing | finding outside authorisation |
| data exposure in the report itself | secrets or personal data copied into the document | redact, reference instead of embed | secret pattern in a report |
