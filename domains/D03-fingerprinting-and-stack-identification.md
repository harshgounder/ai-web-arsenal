## Decision tree

Machine readable at `trees/D03.json`. This is the rendered view.

- **Do you have an origin, or only a DNS name?**
  - _why:_ A DNS name gives you vhosts and certificates. An origin gives you behaviour. The strongest identification comes from combining them, and most people stop at headers.
- `DNS name only`
  - action: certificate transparency for names, then vhost probing, then resolve each to a live origin
  - tools: `d03-ffuf`, `d03-httpx`
  - oracle: `additional names or vhosts that answer`
- `An origin`
  - action: headers, then bundle dependencies, then a second engine. if the three disagree, the bundle is usually right
  - tools: `d03-httpx`, `d03-rea-bundle`, `d03-whatweb`
  - oracle: `three sources agreeing on a stack`
- `A SPA or a bundle`
  - action: fingerprint from dependencies, not headers. headers for a SPA are usually the CDN
  - tools: `d03-rea-bundle`, `d03-wappalyzer`
  - oracle: `framework and version from bundle evidence`
- `A WAF in front`
  - action: fingerprint the WAF first, because it determines which evasion class applies to everything else
  - tools: `d03-wafw00f`
  - oracle: `WAF product identified`
