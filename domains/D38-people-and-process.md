### Details that matter

**Offboarding access review**  
- preconditions: `a person has left or changed role in the last quarter`, `an inventory of systems exists or can be built`
- oracle: `a credential or session still working after the last day, evidenced by a login record`, `every system on the inventory answered for, positive or negative`, `a shared credential rotated, because revoking one account does not revoke a shared secret`

**Shared account and attribution audit**  
- preconditions: `the organisation has accounts used by more than one person`
- oracle: `an action in the log that cannot be attributed to one human`, `one credential used from several distinct clients in a window`, `an access review that lists a person against an account they do not uniquely hold`

**Credential storage outside a manager**  
- preconditions: `the organisation has a password manager or policy`
- oracle: `a working credential found outside the manager, with its location`, `the manager's coverage reported as a fraction of the credential inventory, not a yes`

**Device trust enrollment path**  
- preconditions: `access depends on device posture or enrollment`
- oracle: `an unmanaged device treated as trusted, with the enrollment record or its absence`, `work data present on a device outside management, demonstrated on a real device`

**Privileged access approval trail**  
- preconditions: `the organisation has administrative access to production or data`
- oracle: `an elevated session with no approval record`, `the approval record missing a second party, where dual control is claimed`, `duration of the elevation longer than the task required`

**Disclosure programme scope and safe harbour**  
- preconditions: `the organisation receives external reports`
- oracle: `a report marked out of scope for a real issue, which is a scope defect`, `the safe harbour statement read for what it actually protects`, `acknowledgement measured against the stated window on a real report`

**Review depth on security relevant changes**  
- preconditions: `the project has a review process and code ownership`
- oracle: `a sensitive change merged with one approval and no security review`, `a sensitive file with no named owner`, `the repeat rate of the same class of mistake, which is the only honest measure of whether review teaches`

**Credential lifecycle: reset authority, handover, support view**  
- preconditions: `the organisation has a support desk and onboarding`
- oracle: `a reset completed by a single actor without a second approval, from the audit record`, `an initial password visible to a third party in transit`, `a support view exposing fields unrelated to the request`

**Developer feedback loop and escalation reach**  
- preconditions: `the organisation runs a review process and receives reports`
- oracle: `the same class of mistake recurring, measured per team across engagements`, `time from report to a human acknowledgement, including out of hours`, `a finding that reached nobody who could act on it`
