## Decision tree

Machine readable at `trees/D22.json`. This is the rendered view.

- **Which defence level are you actually facing?**
  - _why:_ Mapped to the defence scale in the ontology: F2 signature, F3 protocol aware, F4 in app, F5 bot management, F6 identity, F7 model based. Each needs a different approach and most of them need no payload at all.
- `F2 signature WAF`
  - action: encoding and syntax mutation against the class, one axis at a time so you learn which axis is filtered
  - tools: `d22-mitmproxy`, `d22-ffuf`
  - oracle: `payload passes where a control is blocked`
- `F3 protocol aware`
  - action: semantic bypass, not encoding. the parser difference between two layers is the opening
  - tools: `d22-nuclei`
  - oracle: `two layers disagree about the same request`
- `F5 bot management`
  - action: differential test: same request from a plain client and a real browser profile. if the answers differ, the defence is the client
  - tools: `d22-curl_cffi`, `d22-playwright`, `d22-undetected-chromedriver`
  - oracle: `response obtained only with a browser shaped client`
- `F7 model based or rate based`
  - action: pace deliberately and keep a request budget. the goal is a result inside the budget, not speed
  - tools: `d22-ffuf`
  - oracle: `results obtained without a block`
- `A challenge (turnstile class)`
  - action: stop and report it. solving that is out of scope for an assessment and is a business decision, not a technical one
  - oracle: `n/a`
