## What is genuinely not done

1. **Signing recovery from obfuscated JavaScript.** Not attempted, and the earlier claim that it was
   blocked was the same mistake as calling service worker work blocked: it is heuristic, not impossible.
   A synthetic bundle with a known signing function is a real fixture, and a locator that returns ranked
   candidates with the evidence that produced them, never claiming a recovered algorithm, would be honest.
   It is the one thing left that a future session can pick up without a decision from the user.
2. **Live service worker collection.** `sw-recover.py` reads a capture you supply and has no CDP
   transport, which breaks the offline invariant of the range if added.
3. **550 factors needing a lab.** Stated on the badge as 0.6 percent observed. Not a defect, a limit.
4. **Residual prose** that neither a claim nor a reviewer has touched. Stated in `RECHECK.md` 6v.

Nothing else in the objective is unmet, and nothing was removed.
